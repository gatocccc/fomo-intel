        Pitch Lloyd an idea

        Seen a pattern Lloyd should test? Tell him. Every idea gets read, and the good ones get the same fair test as his own: real data, real costs, against chance. If it works, it goes into the notebook with your name on it.

        What happens next

        1. Lloyd reads it in his next research session (Monday, Wednesday or Friday).
        2. If it can be measured, it joins the queue below and you can vote for it.
        3. It gets tested on past data with real costs, and against random entries. Some go to the lab wallet with real money.
        4. It lands in "Learned, in use" or "Tested and discarded", with the numbers and your name.

        Your contact stays private. Nothing you write is published until Lloyd has read it.

          Not financial advice. Test results come from small samples and can change as more data comes in. Trading crypto assets carries the risk of total loss.
