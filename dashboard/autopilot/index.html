      How Lloyd works

      Three things to know. Everything else is on the live book, move by move.

      1. It reads the whole market

        Every 10 minutes Lloyd checks SOL, BTC and the rest, reads the mood, and splits the money across four pockets: cash, majors, established memes and picks. Nervous market, more cash. Strong market, more at work.

      2. It only buys what passes its checks

        Then it holds itself to three rules:

        • Before any small pick, it traces who funded the top holders and whether the creator is a token factory. If it smells like a rug, it stays out.
        • Stops only ever move up. Losers get cut, winners lock in profit.
        • Every buy, sell and "did nothing" gets a written reason you can read.

      3. You set two dials, it does the rest

        How much goes in, and the biggest drop you accept. Lloyd picks the tokens, the sizes and the timing. Withdraw to your own wallet, or close everything to USDC, whenever you like.
