A night at the tavern

Six steps, from the door to the bar and back out with your money.

  1. Make an account

    An email and a password. No app to install, no chat group to join.

  2. Pick a goal and a table

    Say how much you want to make, and by when. The odds light shows how likely each plan is to get you there, and how likely it is to cost you half, before you put in a cent.

  3. Get your own wallet

    One wallet per person, however many ducks you hold. The bot can swap inside it, and nothing else.

  4. Fund it, let it trade

    It buys, sells, raises its stops and rotates out of what goes flat, following the plan you chose.

  5. Watch from the bar

    Every buy and every sell shows up in your dashboard with the reasoning behind it, as it happens.

  6. Leave when you like

    Withdraw to your own wallet, pause the bot, or sell everything on the spot.

House rules

Most of us lose for the same three reasons: we fall for a token, we move the stop, and we never check who is behind it. The bot skips all three.

It checks who is behind it

Before buying, it traces who funded the top holders, whether the creator is a token factory, whether bundlers are in, and whether it can actually sell. If it smells like a rug, it stays out.

It never falls in love

When a position is winning, it raises the stop to lock in profit. If the creator sells or liquidity leaves, it walks out without waiting.

It keeps your money working

If a position goes flat and something better shows up, it rotates. If nothing is good enough, it holds cash and tells you why.

It remembers every bad night

Every trade is logged with what the bot knew when it went in. Every loss gets a cause and a cost, and a new rule is tested before it touches your money.

Your money stays in your pocket

The bot can buy and sell. It cannot withdraw, and it cannot send your funds anywhere.

  • Swaps only. Everything it buys lands back in your own wallet.
  • Your limit. You choose how much you are willing to lose. If it gets there, everything moves to USDC and the bot stops.
  • Panic button. Sell everything at once, from your dashboard.
  • Nothing under the counter. Every move, with its reasoning, where you can see it.

Before you pull up a stool

You can lose

Memecoins move hard in both directions. The bot does not guarantee profits, and no honest tool can. An edge means better odds than walking in blind, not a sure thing.

It is still learning

The bot is new and changes every week. Coming in early means trading with a system that is still finding its feet, and taking the risk that comes with that.

Stops can slip

In a sudden crash, a sell set at −30% can fill lower. That is why the bot spreads risk across several positions.

Odds are estimates

They come from simulating thousands of paths with the bot's own results. While real trades are few, they are preliminary, and the odds light says so.
