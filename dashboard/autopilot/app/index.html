Reading the room
    Open the full bookThe notebook

    Nonco Genesis holder?

    Holders pay no fee on profits and get priority seats. Sign a message with the Ethereum wallet that holds your Canvas or Legacy duck. It moves no funds and costs no gas. One person, one account, however many ducks you hold.

    The red button

    Sells every position in your bot wallet to USDC and stops Lloyd on it. It asks once, here, then does it.

    Your dials

    Lloyd picks the tokens, the sizes and the timing. You decide these three things.

    1How much

    From US$20. Add or withdraw whenever you like.

    2Your risk: the biggest drop you accept

    It sets how much of your money follows the book and how tight Lloyd cuts. If your wallet gets there, everything moves to USDC and Lloyd stops on it.

    3What happens with profits

    At that multiple Lloyd moves your original stake to USDC and keeps trading only with the profit.

    Once a month, any profit above your stake is sent to the Solana wallet below. The stake keeps working. This needs a withdrawal wallet.

    Close everything?

    Lloyd will sell every open position in your bot wallet to USDC at market, right now, and stop trading on it. Fees apply to the sells like any other trade.

    Your money stays in your wallet. You can withdraw it, or set your dials and start Lloyd again later.
